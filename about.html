I'm Christo Praveen Kumar, a third-year Information Technology student in Chennai.

Most of my time goes into two things that look very different but start from the same place: building software that is useful to real people, and writing stories that say something about the world around us.

On the tech side I work on AI/ML, full-stack apps and interface design, including my current AI/ML internship at Nokia. On the creative side I write original scripts about climate awareness, mental health, ethical responsibility and psychological suspense.

The two overlap more than you would expect. My Emergency-Response Metaverse is about getting people out of a fire safely, and my script Jeevan is about surviving the aftermath of a flood. Both ask the same question: what helps a person when things go wrong?

I'm comfortable taking the lead: setting direction, keeping a team moving and seeing an idea through to the end. I'm working toward a director role.
